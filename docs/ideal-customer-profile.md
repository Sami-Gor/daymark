# Daymark — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Product:** Daymark — Weather without the noise  
**Website:** https://daymark-weather.pages.dev

## 1. Our entry wedge

**People who check the weather regularly and want a simpler, more private experience.**

They want clear, useful forecasts without clutter, advertising or unnecessary tracking.

## 2. Our ideal customer statement

Our ideal customer is **a regular weather-app user who values simplicity and privacy**, who is frustrated by cluttered apps and unnecessary tracking, currently relies on mainstream weather apps or online searches, and is motivated to find a better alternative because they want quick, clear forecasts without sacrificing their privacy.

## 3. Can we find 10 this week?

**Likely yes — to be tested.**

Potential sources include friends, colleagues, online communities and privacy-conscious technology groups.

The goal is to speak to ten people about how they currently check the weather, what frustrates them, and whether simplicity and privacy would motivate them to try Daymark.

If finding ten suitable people proves difficult, narrow the audience to a specific community or group.
