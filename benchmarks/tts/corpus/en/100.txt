A weak front will move eastwards tonight, bringing a chance of showers between six and nine o'clock. Rainfall should be light and scattered, with most places receiving less than two millimetres. The wind will gradually turn westerly and increase to twenty-five kilometres per hour, with occasional gusts near forty kilometres per hour on higher ground. Humidity is currently seventy-two percent, so it may feel a little cooler than the thermometer suggests. Tomorrow starts cloudy but largely dry, with a minimum of -3 degrees and a maximum of fifteen degrees. A few brighter spells are possible in the afternoon, especially inland.
