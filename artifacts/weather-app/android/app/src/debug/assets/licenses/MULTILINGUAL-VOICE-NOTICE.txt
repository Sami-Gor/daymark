Daymark multilingual narration voices — debug POC only

This file is packaged only in debug builds. It is not present in a production
release, and French or Spanish narration is not available in a production
release. See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and Apache-2.0.txt in this directory.

ff_siwis (French)
-----------------
Voice style: ff_siwis. Source corpus: The SIWIS French Speech Synthesis
Database (2016) by Junichi Yamagishi, Pierre-Edouard Honnet, Philip Garner and
Alexandros Lazaridis, University of Edinburgh, School of Informatics, The
Centre for Speech Technology Research, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/1705
(DataShare record 10283/2353), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International licence, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

The voice style is published by hexgrad in Kokoro-82M,
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, and converted to ONNX .bin by
onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX,
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX. Daymark
redistributes the exact .bin file unmodified; the only known transformation is
the upstream voice-style conversion. Daymark does not claim that the voice or
corpus was modified for Daymark. Redistributed file SHA-256:
a35f5675ad08948e326ae75fd0ea16ba5d0042e4f76b5f3d1df77d0a48c54861.
